## Intended use

BitSend moves music and video you already lawfully have from one of your own
machines to another, within your own home network, for your own private,
noncommercial use. That is the kind of use sometimes called
**space-shifting** ([Wikipedia: Ripping — legality](https://en.wikipedia.org/wiki/Ripping#Legality)):
moving your own copy between your own devices. The law on space-shifting,
and on making personal copies from discs in particular, varies by country.
What you put into BitSend is your responsibility, not the project's.

## What BitSend does not do

- **It does not circumvent DRM.** BitSend transfers files as they are. It
  does not decrypt, bypass, or remove any technological protection measure.
- **It does not download or share content.** BitSend has no ability to fetch
  content from the internet, and its BitTorrent transfer cannot reach a
  public swarm: torrents are created with no trackers and the private flag
  set, and the seed daemon disables DHT, local peer discovery, and port
  mapping. Making files available to anyone outside your own household is
  neither supported nor condoned.

## Summary

Used as designed — your own files, for yourself, kept within your own

sharing files beyond your household or exposing the swarm to the internet,
